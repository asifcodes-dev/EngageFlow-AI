1. Who we are

EngageFlow AI (“EngageFlow”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) provides an AI-powered platform that helps businesses automate Instagram comment replies and direct messages, moderate spam, and capture leads. This Privacy Policy applies to our website, dashboard, and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Account details — name, email address, phone number, and business name when you sign up.
  • Billing details — handled by our payment processor; we store only the plan, status, and transaction references, not your full card details.
  • Support communications — messages you send us by email, chat, or other channels.

2.2 Instagram & Meta data

When you connect an Instagram Professional account through Meta, you authorise us to access only the data needed to run your automations, such as:

  • Your account profile and the posts/reels you choose to automate.
  • Comments and direct messages relevant to your active automations, so our AI can reply on your behalf.
  • Basic engagement metrics used to show you performance in the dashboard.

We access this data via Meta's official APIs and only with the permissions you grant. You can revoke access at any time from your Instagram/Facebook settings or by disconnecting the account in your EngageFlow dashboard.

2.3 Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser information, IP address, and approximate location.
  • Usage data such as pages viewed, features used, and timestamps.
  • Cookies and similar technologies used to keep you signed in and to improve the Service.

3. How we use your information

  • To operate, maintain, and provide the Service, including generating AI replies and moderating comments.
  • To process payments and manage your subscription.
  • To send you transactional messages, product updates, and (with your consent) marketing.
  • To provide support and respond to your requests.
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and security issues.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. AI processing

EngageFlow uses third-party large language models (such as Google Gemini) to generate replies and classify messages. Relevant message content may be sent to these providers solely to produce a response for your account. We do not sell this content, and we configure our integrations to avoid using your data to train third-party models where that option is available.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers — hosting, AI, payment, analytics, and communication partners who process data on our behalf under contract.
  • Meta — as required to operate the Instagram integration you enabled.
  • Legal and safety — when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the Service.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

6. Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain it to meet legal, tax, or accounting obligations.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location and applicable law (including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@engageflow.ai.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures — encryption in transit, access controls, and regular reviews — to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and revise the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will be communicated through the Service or by email.
